# From Weave

A check next to what really comes from Weave: a person, a project or community, or a post that the maintainers of the Weave project say is Weave's (`weave official <who>`). Without it, a look-alike of Weave can't be told apart at a glance.

A mark service Weave ships (docs/v2-stage19.md): it folds `weave.official` events, and only those Weave marks as said in the Weave project by its maintainers (`home`).
