# Websites

A way to join in with the project's participants: the pages its services serve, and programs of yours, as websites everyone here can visit.

It ships with Weave and hosts a project's websites (`websites: host` in its contract): Weave holds the visitors' connections and lets in only who may visit, under its key. It lists them for everyone and lets maintainers close them. It never opens a port itself: a program of yours on this computer is yours to open (Open a website…), and a service opens only its own page.
