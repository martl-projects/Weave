# Badges

Shows the badges someone holds next to their name: each badge's symbol or image, with what it's for and who gave it on hover. Badges are checked as Weave checks them (core's `badgesOf`): given by a maintainer of the series' project, numbered, passed on by their holder.

A mark service Weave ships (docs/v2-stage19.md): it folds `weave.badge.series`, `weave.badge`, `weave.badge.transfer` and `weave.badge.note`, and answers for people (`did`).
